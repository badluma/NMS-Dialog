### Story and Gameplay
This game is a simple, text based game for the 3DS that is inspired by the game No Man's Sky.
It follows the story of a traveller that woke up in the middle of nowhere and is trying to get into space. The player can interact with the story by making decisions, pressing buttons to interact with the environment and more.

### Installation
To install this game on your 3DS, download the ```NMS-Dialog.3dsx``` file and drag it into the ```/3ds``` folder on your SD-Card. After you have done that, turn on your 3DS and launch the Homebrew Launcher. Once the app opened, scroll down with the D-Pad until you see an app called NMS-Dialog. Open it with A and the app will launch.
